COMMON QUESTIONS

What affects a cloud quotation?

What is included in each plan?

The published plan descriptions are indicative. Confirm the full service configuration, support, and operational scope with the team.

Are prices fixed?

No. Published prices are indicative and may vary by provider, configuration, software package, support requirements, and partner arrangements.

Is application deployment included?

Deployment options depend on the application and selected plan. Discuss application requirements before confirming a quotation.

What determines GPU pricing?

GPU pricing is custom and depends on the selected infrastructure environment, capacity, and workload requirements.

Can existing applications be migrated?

Migration can be assessed based on the source environment, application dependencies, and destination requirements.

What backup and support options are available?

Backup and support arrangements depend on the plan and service configuration. Confirm the available options and responsibilities with the team.

How do I request a custom quotation?

Contact the cloud team with your application, infrastructure, and operational requirements.
