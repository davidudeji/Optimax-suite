# IMPLEMENTATION SPECIFICATION: OPTIMAX INTERACTIVE ERP MODULE ECOSYSTEM

## 1. Project Objective

Redesign the existing OptimaX ERP modules section into a visually compelling, interactive and professionally engineered circular ecosystem.

The central design concept is:

**OptimaX is the central business management hub. Every ERP module is an interconnected part of that ecosystem.**

The OptimaX logo must sit at the exact centre of the composition, with all available ERP modules distributed around it in a circular orbit.

The result should communicate that businesses can manage their entire operations through one unified platform.

This is a redesign of the existing modules section, not a redesign of the entire website.

## 2. Design References

Use the following references:

- Original uploaded screenshot: Existing OptimaX modules section.
- Generated circular ecosystem concept: Visual direction for the new layout.

The generated concept establishes the desired composition. Improve its visual execution rather than reproducing it literally.

### Core design principles

- Enterprise-grade visual quality.
- Minimalist, modern SaaS aesthetic.
- Strong visual hierarchy.
- Balanced circular composition.
- Generous whitespace.
- Subtle shadows and borders.
- Consistent iconography.
- Smooth, purposeful interactions.
- No unnecessary decorative elements.
- No generic AI-generated website appearance.

## 3. Layout Architecture

Replace the existing two-column layout with a centrally aligned composition.

### Section header

Position the heading and description at the top centre of the section.

Headline:

"Power every part of your business."

Supporting text:

"Explore our integrated ERP modules designed to connect your finance, people, sales and operations. Discover how every part of your business works better together."

Typography:

- Large, bold headline.
- Dark navy primary text.
- Use a subtle blue or teal accent on selected words.
- Supporting text should be muted blue-grey.
- Limit paragraph width for readability.
- Centre-align all text.

### Circular ecosystem

The circular ecosystem must be the primary visual element.

Structure:

- Central OptimaX logo.
- Inner circular orbit surrounding the logo.
- Outer circular orbit containing the ERP modules.
- Eight module positions distributed evenly around the centre.
- Thin, subtle orbit lines connecting the composition visually.

The composition must remain geometrically balanced.

Do not position modules randomly or use arbitrary spacing.

## 4. Central OptimaX Hub

The centre must contain the official OptimaX logo and wordmark.

Content:

OPTIMAX

One Platform. Your Entire Business, Connected.

Design requirements:

- Prominent circular container.
- White background.
- Official brand logo.
- Subtle shadow.
- Fine border.
- Soft blue/teal accent around the perimeter.
- Slightly elevated appearance compared to the surrounding modules.

The centre must remain the most visually dominant element.

Interaction:

- Keep the central logo stationary.
- Avoid excessive animation.
- A very subtle breathing glow is optional.
- The logo must never rotate with the orbit.

## 5. ERP Module Distribution

Arrange eight modules around the central hub.

Each module must occupy its own circular position around the central logo.

Modules:

1. Finance Management
2. Human Resources
3. Sales Management
4. Inventory Management
5. Supply Chain Management
6. Manufacturing
7. Commerce Management
8. Customer Relationship Management (CRM)

Use the existing website's module names and descriptions wherever they already exist. Do not invent new capabilities or remove existing module information.

### Module presentation

Each module should be represented by a circular or softly rounded visual card containing:

- A relevant module image or visual.
- Module title.
- Short supporting description.
- Interactive hover state.

Example:

**Finance Management**

Manage financial records, expenses and business reporting.

**Human Resources**

Organize employee information, attendance, leave and payroll.

**Sales Management**

Manage sales activities, orders and customer transactions.

**Inventory Management**

Track stock levels, warehouse activities and inventory movements.

**Supply Chain Management**

Coordinate procurement, vendors and supply chain operations.

**Manufacturing**

Manage production activities and manufacturing operations.

**Commerce Management**

Manage point-of-sale activities and commercial operations.

**Customer Relationship Management**

Organize customer information and strengthen business relationships.

These descriptions are introductory copy. Preserve more detailed existing descriptions on the relevant product pages.

## 6. Module Visual Design

Each module should have a consistent visual structure.

Recommended appearance:

- Circular image or icon container.
- Module title directly beneath the visual.
- Short description below the title.
- White or very light background.
- Subtle border.
- Soft shadow.
- Consistent internal spacing.

Use actual OptimaX product screenshots or existing module-specific visual assets where available.

If screenshots are unavailable, use professionally designed Lucide icons as temporary visual representations.

Do not use unrelated stock photography.

All eight modules must follow the same visual system.

## 7. Interactive Behaviour

Interaction is a critical requirement.

### A. Hover interaction

When a visitor hovers over a module:

- Increase its scale slightly.
- Elevate the card visually.
- Introduce a subtle shadow.
- Highlight its border with the brand accent colour.
- Reveal additional module information through a smooth transition.
- Make the corresponding orbit connection more visible.

Animation should feel smooth and restrained.

Suggested timing: 200–350ms.

Avoid aggressive bouncing, spinning or distracting transitions.

### B. Click interaction

Every module must be clickable.

Clicking a module should navigate to its existing dedicated product page.

Use the website's existing routes.

Do not create duplicate pages or change established URL structures.

### C. Active module state

When a module is selected:

- Highlight the selected module.
- Give its orbit connection a stronger visual emphasis.
- Keep the remaining modules visually available.
- Ensure the central OptimaX logo remains visible.

### D. Keyboard interaction

- Modules must be keyboard accessible.
- Provide visible focus states.
- Use semantic links or buttons appropriately.
- Support Enter and Space where applicable.
- Provide meaningful accessible labels.

## 8. Animation and Motion

Use purposeful animations to make the ecosystem feel connected.

Suggested sequence on initial page load:

1. Section heading fades in.
2. Supporting description appears.
3. Central OptimaX logo scales into view.
4. Circular orbit lines reveal themselves.
5. Surrounding modules appear sequentially with subtle fade-and-scale animations.

Additional motion:

- Subtle hover elevation.
- Smooth module transitions.
- Optional gentle orbit highlight animation.

Important:
The modules themselves should NOT continuously rotate around the logo.

The orbit lines should remain stable. The composition should feel professional rather than like a science-fiction animation.

Respect prefers-reduced-motion settings.

## 9. Visual Design System

Use the established OptimaX brand identity.

Primary colours:

- Deep Navy: #0B1B33
- White: #FFFFFF
- Teal: Use the existing official brand accent.
- Light backgrounds: Subtle blue-grey tones.

Visual rules:

- No excessive gradients.
- No random colours for individual modules.
- No excessive rounded rectangles.
- No decorative floating badges.
- No unnecessary labels.
- No oversized icons.
- No inconsistent shadows.
- No visual clutter.

Use consistent spacing, typography, border radii and icon sizes.

The composition must feel like a premium enterprise software product.
