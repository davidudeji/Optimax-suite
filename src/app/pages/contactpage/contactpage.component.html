Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before getting started with OptimaX.

How quickly can we get OptimaX up and running?
Most businesses are fully live within 2 weeks. Our implementation team handles data migration, configuration, and staff training. For enterprise deployments with complex workflows, we typically allow 4–6 weeks. You'll have a dedicated onboarding manager from day one.
Do you offer a free trial or demo before committing?
Yes — we offer a fully guided 14-day free trial with access to all modules. You can also book a personalised live demo with one of our product specialists who will walk you through the exact workflows relevant to your industry. No credit card required.
Can we migrate our existing data from spreadsheets or another ERP?
Absolutely. We support data migration from Excel/CSV files, QuickBooks, Sage, Odoo, and most other ERP systems via our structured import tools. Our team will validate and clean your data before it goes live, ensuring zero data loss and full historical continuity.
