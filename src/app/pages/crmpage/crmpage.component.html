Core Features

Everything your sales team needs to win

From first contact to closed deal and beyond, OptimaX CRM provides a complete toolkit that keeps your sales team focused on selling — not on admin.

Contact Management

Store and organise all contacts and company records in a single, searchable database. Capture every interaction — calls, emails, meetings, notes — against the right contact. Tag, segment, and filter your database to identify and prioritise the right opportunities quickly.

Sales Pipeline

Visualise your entire deal flow on a customisable Kanban-style pipeline board. Drag deals between stages, set weighted values, and get an instant read on your pipeline health and projected close dates. Multiple pipelines support different products or sales teams.

Task & Activity Log

Assign tasks to team members, set due dates, and link activities to deals and contacts. Automated reminders ensure no follow-up is forgotten. Every call made, email sent, and meeting held is logged automatically — giving managers full visibility into team activity.

Email Integration

Connect your work email to OptimaX CRM so all correspondence with contacts is automatically captured and linked to the right deal record. Send emails directly from the CRM, use personalised templates, and track open and reply rates — all without switching tabs.

Sales Forecasting

Generate revenue forecasts based on deal stages, weighted probabilities, and close date projections. Compare forecasts against targets by rep, team, or region. Give leadership the forward visibility they need to plan headcount, inventory, and cash flow confidently.

Reports & Dashboards

Access a live sales performance dashboard with metrics on pipeline value, conversion rates, average deal size, and team leaderboards. Build custom reports for any timeframe, segment, or KPI — then share them with stakeholders in one click.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about OptimaX CRM

Can I import my existing contact data from another CRM?
Yes. OptimaX CRM supports bulk import via CSV for contacts, companies, and deal records. Our onboarding team assists with field mapping so your data lands correctly on the first import. We also offer migration support for teams moving from Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho, and similar platforms.
How many sales pipelines can I create?
You can create an unlimited number of pipelines on the Business and Enterprise plans. Each pipeline has fully customisable stages, probability settings, and required fields. This allows different teams — new business, renewals, partnerships — to each manage their own workflow without conflict.
Does the CRM integrate with OptimaX Accounting?
Yes. When a deal is marked as won in OptimaX CRM, you can convert it directly into a customer invoice in the Accounting module without re-entering any data. Customer records stay in sync between both modules, and revenue from closed deals feeds directly into your financial dashboards.
Is there a limit on the number of contacts I can store?
The Starter plan includes up to 5,000 contacts. Business and Enterprise plans offer unlimited contacts and companies. All plans include unlimited deal records, activity logs, and notes. Storage for email attachments and documents counts against your plan's file storage quota.
