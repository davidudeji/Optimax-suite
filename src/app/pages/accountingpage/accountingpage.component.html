Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about OptimaX Accounting

Does OptimaX Accounting support multi-currency transactions?
Yes. OptimaX Accounting supports multiple currencies with configurable exchange rates. All foreign currency transactions are automatically converted to your base currency for reporting, and unrealised forex gains or losses are tracked and reported separately in compliance with IAS 21.
Can I migrate data from my existing accounting software?
Absolutely. Our onboarding team provides guided data migration from popular platforms including QuickBooks, Sage, and Xero. We import your chart of accounts, opening balances, vendor/customer records, and historical transactions so you can hit the ground running from day one.
Is the system compliant with local tax regulations?
OptimaX Accounting is built to accommodate country-specific tax rules including VAT, WHT, and corporate income tax configurations. Tax codes and rates are fully configurable by your admin, and the system generates the schedules and summaries needed for regulatory filings.
How does OptimaX Accounting integrate with other modules?
The Accounting module is tightly integrated with OptimaX Procurement, Payroll, POS, and Expense Management. Transactions initiated in any of those modules automatically generate the appropriate journal entries in the general ledger — eliminating duplicate data entry across your business.
