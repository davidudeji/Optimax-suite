Core Features

Built for employees. Controlled by finance.

OptimaX Expense Management makes submitting claims effortless for employees while giving finance complete visibility, control, and automated compliance enforcement.

Expense Submission

Employees submit expenses on any device with a clean, guided form. Categorise expenses, attach receipts, enter amounts in any currency, and add notes — all in under two minutes. Submissions are automatically routed to the correct approver without manual forwarding.

Approval Workflows

Configure multi-level approval chains by department, expense type, or amount threshold. Approvers receive real-time notifications and can approve or reject with a single tap — from desktop or mobile. Escalation rules prevent claims from sitting unreviewed.

Receipt Scanning

Snap a photo of any receipt with a smartphone camera. OptimaX extracts merchant name, date, and amount automatically, pre-filling the expense form. Receipts are stored securely and linked to each claim for audit purposes — eliminating paper filing permanently.

Policy Enforcement

Define spend limits, allowable categories, and meal caps per role or grade. The system automatically flags or blocks out-of-policy claims before they reach approvers, so your expense policy is enforced consistently without any manual review effort.

Mileage & Per Diem

Log business trips and calculate mileage reimbursement using configurable rate cards. Set daily per diem allowances by location, seniority, or trip duration. All calculations are automatic, consistent, and fully auditable.

Integration with Payroll

Approved expense claims sync directly with OptimaX Payroll for reimbursement in the next payroll run. No re-entering data, no delays, no disconnected systems. Employees get reimbursed on time, every time, with a full paper trail for finance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about OptimaX Expense Management

Can employees submit expenses on mobile devices?
Yes. The OptimaX Expense Management interface is fully responsive and optimised for mobile browsers. Employees can capture receipts, complete submission forms, and track reimbursement status entirely from their smartphone — no separate app download required.
How are out-of-policy expenses handled?
When an expense violates a configured policy rule — such as exceeding a meal cap or using a disallowed category — the system immediately flags it with a clear explanation. Depending on your settings, the claim can be blocked outright or passed to a senior approver for exception handling. All exceptions are logged for audit review.
Does OptimaX Expense handle foreign currency claims?
Yes. Employees travelling internationally can submit expenses in any currency. The system converts amounts to the base currency using either the rate on the transaction date or a configurable company rate. The original foreign currency amount and rate are always preserved for reference.
How long does it take to set up expense policies?
Most organisations configure their core expense policies in under an hour using the admin settings panel. Our onboarding team provides a guided setup session to help you translate your existing expense policy document into system rules. Changes can be made at any time without technical assistance.
