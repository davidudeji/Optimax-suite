Core Features

Total control of every purchase

OptimaX Procurement gives your team the tools to manage the entire source-to-pay cycle with structure, discipline, and complete transparency.

Purchase Requisition

Staff submit structured purchase requests with item details, justification, and budget codes. Requests are routed automatically to the right approvers based on amount thresholds and department rules configured by your admin.

Supplier Management

Maintain a verified supplier database with contact details, payment terms, compliance documents, and performance ratings. Run supplier onboarding workflows and evaluate vendor reliability through historical transaction data and scorecards.

Purchase Orders

Generate professional purchase orders from approved requisitions in one click. POs are emailed directly to suppliers with line item details, delivery timelines, and payment terms. Track open POs and outstanding deliveries in real time.

Goods Received Notes

Record the receipt of goods against outstanding POs. Capture partial deliveries, flag quality discrepancies, and automatically update inventory levels. GRNs form the basis for three-way matching before vendor invoice payment is approved.

Budget Controls

Set department-level and project-level budgets. The system checks available budget at requisition stage and blocks or flags over-budget requests before any commitment is made. Real-time budget utilisation dashboards keep finance teams in control.

Audit Trail

Every action — from requisition creation to PO approval and goods receipt — is logged with a user timestamp and reason. Procurement audits become straightforward exercises in pulling reports, not manual investigations across paper trails.

How It Works

From request to receipt in four steps

OptimaX Procurement imposes a clear, structured workflow that keeps every purchase accountable and visible from start to finish.

1

Request

A team member submits a purchase requisition with item description, quantity, estimated cost, and the budget code to charge. The request is validated against available budget instantly.

2

Approve

The requisition is routed through a configurable approval chain. Approvers receive instant notifications and can approve, reject, or query requests from their desktop or mobile device.

3

Order

Once approved, the procurement team selects a verified supplier and raises a formal purchase order. The PO is dispatched electronically and tracked until the supplier acknowledges receipt.

4

Receive

The receiving team logs a Goods Received Note against the PO. Inventory is updated automatically and the accounts payable module is triggered for three-way matching and payment processing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about OptimaX Procurement

Can I set different approval thresholds per department?
Yes. Approval rules are fully configurable per department, cost centre, and spend category. You can define up to three levels of approval and set monetary thresholds for each level. Rules can be updated at any time by your system administrator without needing technical support.
Does OptimaX Procurement integrate with inventory management?
Yes. When a Goods Received Note is posted, inventory quantities are automatically updated in the OptimaX inventory module. Reorder points can be configured to automatically trigger purchase requisitions when stock falls below defined minimum levels.
How does three-way matching work in OptimaX?
The system automatically compares the Purchase Order, Goods Received Note, and vendor invoice before flagging a payment for approval. Discrepancies in quantity, price, or terms are highlighted and routed back for resolution — preventing overpayments and duplicate payments.
Can suppliers access the portal to update delivery status?
Yes. OptimaX provides a supplier portal where vendors can acknowledge purchase orders, update expected delivery dates, submit invoices electronically, and view payment status — all without needing a full OptimaX licence. This reduces back-and-forth emails and keeps your procurement records accurate.
