Core Features of an CRM

The Customer Relationship Management (CRM) in OptimaX ERP helps businesses manage interactions with
customers, streamline sales processes, and improve customer retention.

Customer Support and Service

✓
Track customer service issues and resolution times, ensuring timely responses and customer satisfaction.
✓
Create a self-service portal where customers can find answers to common issues, reducing support load.
✓
Service Level Agreements (SLAs): Define SLAs for different customer tiers, ensuring high-value customers receive the best service.

Reporting and Analytics

✓
Generate detailed reports on sales team performance,
deal success rates, and revenue metrics.
✓
Analyze customer segments to tailor marketing campaigns
and sales strategies for different groups.
✓
Track the success of marketing campaigns by monitoring
engagement rates, leads generated, and conversions.

Lead and Opportunity Management

✓
Capture and organize leads from multiple channels (website, social media, referrals) in one centralized system.
✓
Monitor the sales pipeline stages, tracking each opportunity from prospect to closure.
✓
Predict potential revenue by analyzing the progress of leads and their likelihood of converting into sales.
