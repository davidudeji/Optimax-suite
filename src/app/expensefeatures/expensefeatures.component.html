Expense Tracking

✓
Employees can submit expenses in real-time using web or mobile devices, including receipts and details for each expense.
✓
Allows employees to report expenses in different currencies, automatically converting them based on exchange rates.
✓
Expenses can be categorized by type (e.g., travel, meals, office supplies) for better reporting and policy compliance.

Approval Workflow

✓
Submitted expenses are automatically routed to the appropriate manager or department for approval based on predefined rules.
✓
Managers and employees receive notifications about pending approvals or rejected expenses with feedback.
✓
Managers can only approve expenses within their departments, maintaining accountability and transparency.

Reimbursement Management

✓
Once expenses are approved, they can be quickly processed
for reimbursement, either through payroll or direct deposit.
✓
Track and process expense payouts, ensuring timely
reimbursement to employees.
✓
Seamlessly integrates with payroll systems, allowing for accurate
tracking and processing of employee reimbursements.
