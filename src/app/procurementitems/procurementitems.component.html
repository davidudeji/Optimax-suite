Core Features of Procurement in OptimaX ERP

For OptimaX ERP, a detailed approach to procurement involves integrating all aspects of
the purchasing process with your ERP system, creating a unified platform that optimizes
efficiency, reduces errors, and improves cost control.

Supplier Management

Ensures efficient and streamlined interactions with suppliers, enhancing procurement efficiency and fostering strong vendor relationships. The supplier management in OptimaX ERP helps organizations optimize the entire supplier lifecycle, from onboarding to performance evaluation.

Purchase Requisition

This is a streamlined process that allows organizations to create and submit requests for goods or services, automate approval workflows, and integrate with inventory management for accurate demand planning. This system ensures that purchase requests are aligned with stock levels, routed through customizable approval processes, and tracked in real-time, providing full transparency and control.

RFQ Management

This is a comprehensive system for managing the entire procurement bidding process, including the creation and management of Local Purchase Order (LPO) and Requests for Quotes (RFQ), bid evaluation and scoring, and contract award management.It streamlines the process by automating the generation and distribution of LPOs and RFQs to suppliers, allowing procurement teams to easily compare vendor bids based on criteria like price and quality.

Purchase Order

This is a comprehensive system that automates and streamlines the entire procurement lifecycle, including purchase order generation and processing, approval workflows, real-time PO tracking and status updates, as well as goods receipt processing, quality inspection, and variance analysis. It enables seamless creation of purchase orders, ensures timely approvals, and provides full visibility into order statuses.

Receiving and Inspection

This is a vital process that ensures the accurate receipt of goods from suppliers and verifies their quality and quantity before they are accepted into inventory. This module is designed to streamline the receiving workflow, from checking goods against purchase orders to performing quality inspections, ensuring that only compliant items are added to the company’s stock.

Invoice Processing

OptimaX ERP is a fully integrated solution designed to streamline and automate the handling of supplier invoices, from receipt to approval and payment. This process ensures that invoices are accurately verified, matched with purchase orders and goods receipts, and seamlessly integrated into the accounts payable system for timely payment.
